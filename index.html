Service 01

CBAM Compliance for Indian Exporters

Your European customers are legally required to declare the embedded carbon in every tonne of steel, cement, aluminium, or fertiliser they import from India. If you cannot provide verified data, they use EU default values - typically 30% to 80% above your actual emissions.

That gap costs money. Either yours or theirs. And it directly affects whether you remain a preferred supplier.

We calculate your embedded carbon in the EU's exact methodology, coordinate verification, and prepare the complete CBAM data package your European customer needs.

What this includes

  • ✓ Embedded carbon calculation per EU CBAM implementing regulation
  • ✓ Verification coordination with ISO 14065 accredited bodies
  • ✓ CBAM declaration data package preparation
  • ✓ CCC deduction strategy to reduce EU liability
  • ✓ Ongoing annual monitoring and declaration support

Service 02

CCTS and Carbon Market Advisory

India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme is live. 490 industrial facilities are now legally required to measure their emission intensity, submit verified MRV data, and buy or sell Carbon Credit Certificates on Indian power exchanges.

Most of them have never built a GHG inventory before.

We help you define your emission boundary correctly, build your data collection system, calculate your intensity to BEE's exact methodology, prepare for third-party verification, and develop a CCC trading strategy that treats compliance as a financial position - not just a regulatory obligation.

What this includes

  • ✓ Emission boundary definition and GHG inventory development
  • ✓ Monthly data collection system design
  • ✓ BEE-aligned MRV report preparation
  • ✓ Verification coordination with accredited agencies
  • ✓ CCC compliance position modelling and trading strategy
Talk to us about CCTS

BRSR and ESG Reporting

SEBI now requires third-party assurance on BRSR Core disclosures for India's top listed companies. Value chain disclosures are mandatory. The assurance body will ask for data trails, methodology documentation, and evidence of internal controls.

Most companies are not ready for that level of scrutiny.

We help you build a BRSR program that does not just pass compliance - it communicates something real to the investors, customers, and regulators reading it.

What this includes

  • ✓ Materiality assessment grounded in your actual business
  • ✓ Data architecture across HR, operations, finance, and procurement
  • ✓ BRSR Core report drafting and narrative development
  • ✓ Assurance readiness preparation
  • ✓ Value chain ESG data collection from suppliers
Talk to us about BRSR

Service 04

CSRD Gap Assessment for Indian MNCs

Indian multinationals with European subsidiaries or significant EU revenue are entering CSRD scope. The simplified ESRS still requires double materiality assessment, Scope 3 disclosure, and due diligence processes that most Indian companies have never done.

Your BRSR report is a head start. The gap between BRSR and CSRD is smaller than most people think - and larger than most people are prepared for.

We map exactly where you stand, what needs to be built, and in what sequence.

What this includes

  • ✓ BRSR to CSRD gap analysis across all ESRS standards
  • ✓ Double materiality assessment design
  • ✓ Scope 3 value chain emissions mapping
  • ✓ CSRD compliance roadmap and timeline
  • ✓ Ongoing CSRD reporting support
